Tradebook Analyzer

Drop in your Zerodha F&O tradebook (.xlsx or .csv). Each buy and sell is matched into round trips, then checked against your own trading rules. Your file is read in this browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

Your rules

These start as example limits. Change them to your own and everything below recalculates. Charges are optional: the tradebook does not include them, so copy the total from your P&L statement to see net results.
